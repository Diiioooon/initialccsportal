About the System

The CCS Laboratory Sit‑in Monitoring System is a web‑based platform designed to streamline the management of laboratory sessions at the University of Cebu – College of Computer Studies.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide an efficient, transparent, and user‑friendly tool that helps students reserve laboratory slots, track their sit‑in hours, and stay updated with announcements, while allowing administrators to easily manage sessions, resources, and student data.

⚙️ Key Features

✔ Online seat reservation with real‑time conflict detection
✔ Walk‑in sit‑in registration and monitoring
✔ Automated session counting and analytics
✔ Feedback and testimonial collection
✔ Software & hardware inventory management
✔ Dark / light theme toggle for comfortable use

👩‍💻 Developed By

This system was developed as a capstone project by BSIT students of the University of Cebu, under the guidance of the College of Computer Studies faculty.
